> **Pancake v2 update, August 27, 2026:** Pancake now costs $99 per month with every GTM agent included. It is a focused AI GTM team, not the $49 Slack-native general operator described in the original version of this article.

Viktor and Pancake are close enough in entry price to invite comparison, but the products package different outcomes. Viktor starts at $50 per month and presents itself as a broad AI employee. Pancake costs $99 per month and provides a coordinated AI GTM team.

**TL;DR:** Choose Viktor if you want one adaptable AI employee across many internal tasks. Choose Pancake if you want several GTM agents sharing one Brain and executing customer-acquisition workflows from signal through outreach and publication.

## The prices at a glance

| | Viktor | Pancake v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Starting price | From $50/month | $99/month |
| Product model | Broad AI employee | Coordinated AI GTM team |
| Primary scope | Cross-functional assistance and execution | Buying signals, leads, outreach, and AI search |
| Collaboration surface | Slack and Microsoft Teams | Dedicated GTM product |
| Integrations | Broad integration catalog | GTM workflows connected through one Brain |
| Pricing unit | Plan and usage allowances | Every GTM agent included |

Prices and allowances can change. Viktor's own pricing page is the source of truth for its current plan limits. Pancake's current offer is $99 per month with every GTM agent included.

## What Viktor's price buys

Viktor is designed as a general AI employee. Its value is breadth: it can work through team communication tools, connect to a large catalog of applications, perform scheduled work, and support many functions.

That makes the entry price attractive for a team that wants a flexible shared operator. The important purchasing question is not just the sticker price, but whether the relevant plan includes enough usage for the workflows you intend to run.

## What Pancake's price buys

Pancake's $99 monthly price includes the GTM agents. They are not separate assistants with isolated context. They share a GTM Brain containing your positioning, ideal customers, proof, objections, offers, and accumulated results.

The included motion covers:

- monitoring buying signals;
- finding and qualifying warm leads;
- personalized outreach;
- drafting and publishing content for Google and AI search;
- measuring outcomes and improving the shared GTM Brain.

The goal is not access to a generic AI worker. It is a GTM system that keeps executing and learning.

## Why a simple price comparison is misleading

At face value, $50 is less than $99. That only decides the question if both products perform the same job.

For a team that wants help with research, internal coordination, documents, and tasks across many departments, Viktor's breadth can be the better value. For a founder who would otherwise combine a lead-research tool, outreach automation, content production, and AI-search optimization, Pancake consolidates those jobs into one coordinated product.

The more useful comparison is therefore cost per completed motion:

- With Viktor, ask how much configuration and ongoing direction your chosen workflows require.
- With Pancake, ask whether its GTM scope matches the customer-acquisition work you need to run.

## Choose Viktor if

- You need a broad AI employee rather than a GTM-specific system.
- Slack or Microsoft Teams is where you want the product to live.
- A large catalog of general integrations matters most.
- Your team will define and direct workflows across several functions.

## Choose Pancake if

- Customer acquisition is the priority.
- You want specialized agents for signals, leads, outreach, and AI-search content.
- Every agent should learn from one shared GTM Brain.
- You want the product to execute recurring workflows and publish, not only recommend next steps.

## Bottom line

Viktor starts lower and covers a wider range of work. Pancake costs $99 per month and goes deeper on one function: GTM. The right choice depends less on the $49 difference than on whether you want a general AI employee or an autonomous GTM team.
